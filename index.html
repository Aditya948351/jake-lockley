Powerful Core Features

Beyond simple ticketing: a full-spectrum administrative engine.

AI Talking Assistant

Guidance-only conversational AI that helps citizens choose categories, explains status in plain language, and answers FAQs without human intervention.

Community Space

Public issue threads where citizens discuss ward-level problems, reducing duplicate complaints and fostering civic engagement.

SLA Breach Prediction

System flags complaints likely to breach deadlines based on officer backlog and historical seasonal trends (like monsoons).

Evidence-Based Flow

Mandatory photo evidence from officers for resolution and optional video links from citizens ensure verifiable outcomes.

Hotspot Mapping

Data-driven insights to identify recurring issue wards, helping authorities make policy-level resource allocations.

Sovereign Audit Trail

Complete timeline tracking of who handled which ticket, when it was updated, and any AI overrides performed by officers.
